Pain

0 No pain
0: No pain1 to 3: Mild: noticeable, I could do my usual day4 to 6: Moderate: I had to slow down or skip some things7 to 9: Severe: I could not do my usual activities10: Extremely severe

Why: The 0 to 10 menstrual pain scale is validated with the end labels "No pain" and "Extremely severe"; a change of about 1 point is meaningful (NRS). The middle labels tie numbers to daily life so 6 means the same to everyone; those middle wordings are our design choice. Feeds: pain_high signal (context for all patterns)

Why: Function is what matters to a doctor: "missed 3 days of college" is clearer than a number. Feeds: proposed: pain_impact (shown in the doctor visit summary)

Why: A pain score of 3 after a painkiller is not the same as 3 without one. Feeds: proposed: context for pain_high
